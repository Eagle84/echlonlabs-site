Echlon AI is the lab's flagship.

Coding assistants help one person type faster. Echlon AI works like an organisation: it plans the work, splits it up, has it reviewed, and refuses to ship something broken.

Fifty-two roles across eight departments sit behind one request box. Each role names the reviewer who signs its work off, and results are checked against written acceptance criteria before you see them.

  1. Describe the goal in one sentence.
  2. It routes to the department that owns the work.
  3. You approve the plan.
  4. The team builds, and a named reviewer checks the result.
  5. Real lint and test commands decide whether it passes.
  6. You get the code.
